# KPI Definitions

- **Cases:** number of records after selected filters.
- **SLA compliance:** count of cases where resolution hours ≤ priority SLA target divided by case count.
- **Average resolution:** arithmetic mean of resolution hours.
- **SLA thresholds:** High = 8h, Medium = 24h, Low = 48h; fictional policy.

Operational questions: which priority tier has lowest compliance; does channel mix explain volume; which queue needs investigation?
